<h1>Mailing Lists</h1>

There are two mailing lists for SRS.

<dl>
<dt><a href="http://www.listbox.com/subscribe/?listname=srs-discuss">srs-discuss</a></dt>
<dd>The discussion list for all things related to SRS. Talk here.</dd>
<dt><a href="http://www.listbox.com/subscribe/?listname=srs-devel">srs-devel</a></dt>
<dd>The developers list for SRS. Patch here.</dd>
<dt><a href="http://www.libspf2.net/support.html">SPF support lists</a></dt>
<dd>Lists and support for SPF, the other half of the protocol pair.</dd>
<dt><a href="http://spf.pobox.com/mailinglist.html">Other lists at
	pobox.com</a></dt>
<dd>There are several other SPF and SRS related mailing lists, some
	of which are very high volume. Caveat subscriber.</dd>
</dl>
{% endcomment %}
